# Asset placeholders

These folders are reserved for LIST Software assets only after the company grants authorization. No company logo, employee/team photograph, office image, customer logo or award artwork is included in this demo.

- `logo/`: approved corporate logo files
- `company/`: authorized company photography and approved corporate imagery
- `office/`: approved office photography
- `team/`: approved team photography
- `products/`: approved product screenshots or product artwork
- `awards/`: verified, authorized award artwork
- `icons/`: approved brand iconography

The current site uses a CSS-created demonstration wordmark and original interface illustrations instead.
